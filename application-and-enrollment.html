Enrollment for the 2026-2027 school opens January 2026.

Here are the steps to the enrollment process:

Step 1: Contact our director (link to the director email- director.gloriadomini@gmail.com)

Step 2: Complete Family Application

Our family application will be provided to you by the community director via email and will include a family application as well as signatures of both our belief statement and education philosophy.

Step 3: Meet with community director to discuss application

Step 4: Pay enrollment deposits for all applicable programs

Step 5: Finalize enrollment by making payment in full by the applicable deadlines on the application
